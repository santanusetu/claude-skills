# Sample reports

These show tone, length and shape. Every team, player and game here is invented, so never reuse a name or a number from them.

## A close game, with a rival to answer

Tara Menon and Joel Fisher were the 2 contenders for Player of the Match, and Tara's all-round game made the difference in an 8-run win.

Lake Otters won the toss, batted first and were 41/3 when Tara came in. Tara made 31 off 28 and added 38 with Sam Wu for the 4th wicket, which carried the Otters to 112/7.

Joel made the top score of the match for Oak Foxes, 46 off 38, and had them on 78/2 with 35 needed from 30 balls. Tara then bowled Joel, and came back in the 18th over to take 2 more wickets for 3 runs. The Foxes finished on 104/8.

Joel's innings was the best of the day, but Tara's 3/14 from 4 overs turned the chase, and those 31 runs were worth more than the margin. Well played to both sides.

## A clear winner in a low-scoring game

Priya Bose was an easy choice in this one. River Kites chose to bat and struggled throughout, but Priya held one end with 44 off 52, nearly half of their 98 all out. Nobody else passed 14.

Pine Rovers never got going in reply and were all out for 61 in 16.2 overs. The Kites' bowlers shared the wickets, with nobody taking more than 2, so no single spell came close to matching Priya's innings. That innings gave the Kites a total to defend when it looked like they wouldn't have one, and in a game with so few runs, that decided it.

## A pick from the losing side

Dan Okafor earned this award despite finishing on the losing side. Mesa Owls made 134/6, with Rhea Sato's 2/19 for Cove Bears the best of the bowling.

The Bears were 22/4 when Dan walked in, and the game looked over. Dan made 68 off 47, more than half of their final score, and took them to 120/8 with 15 needed from 7 balls before being caught in the deep. The Bears ended on 127/9, just 7 runs short.

The Owls had good contributions all round, but nobody on either side did as much to shape the game. Congratulations to the Owls on the win, and to Dan on a fine innings.
